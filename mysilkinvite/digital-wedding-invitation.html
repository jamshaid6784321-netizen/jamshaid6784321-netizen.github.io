Digital Wedding Invitation

A wedding invitation your guests can touch — not just read. Birds that fly away when tapped, a live countdown, and an RSVP that takes ten seconds. Made in minutes, shared on WhatsApp.

A paper card gets read once and put away. A digital wedding invitation stays on your guests' phones — with the date, the venue, the programme, and a way to reply. My Silk Invite builds it as a single beautiful page that opens from a link or a QR code, on any phone, with no app to install.

What's inside your invitation

  • An interactive card. Golden Birds or Royal Night — tap the birds, butterflies or lanterns and they respond. No video, only code, so it loads fast on a phone.
  • RSVP built in. Guests reply with their name and number of guests. Their replies are collected for you.
  • Live countdown. Days, hours, minutes and seconds to the big day, updating on the page.
  • Add to Calendar. One tap saves the date to your guest's calendar.
  • QR code. Print it on your paper card — guests scan it and the invitation opens.
  • Per-guest links. On Premium, every guest can get their own personal link with their name on it.
  • Both events, one page. Nikah and Walima, each with its own date, time and venue.

How it works

1. Pick a template. Golden Birds for a warm sunset feel, Royal Night for an emerald evening under the moon.

2. Add your details. Names, date, time, venue and your events. The preview updates live as you type.

3. Share it. Copy your link, send it on WhatsApp, or print the QR code on your paper card.
